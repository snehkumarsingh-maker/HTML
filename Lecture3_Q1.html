My favourate place is kashmir (Srinagar)

My journey to Kashmir was an unforgettable experience of heavenly natural beauty, snow-capped mountains, and amazing culture.

Most attracting sites

Srinagar
heart of Kashmir. Here you can enjoy an overnight stay in a houseboat and a traditional Shikara ride on the famous Dal Lake .
Mughal Gardens
: Historic gardens like Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh are full of natural beauty.
Gulmarg
: Known as the 'Meadow of Flowers', this place is known for its snow-capped mountains and the famous Gulmarg Gondola Ride.
Pahalgam
: This beautiful city, situated on the banks of the Leader River, has scenic spots like the Betab Valley and the Aru Valley.
Sonmarg
: Sonmarg, known as the gateway to the Himalayas, is famous for its glaciers and mountains
